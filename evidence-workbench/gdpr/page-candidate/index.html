2 - Applicability / Territorial Reach

Article 3 limbs

Three verified limbs for planning maps: Union establishment (EP-CLM-000018), offering goods/services (EP-CLM-000019), and monitoring behaviour (EP-CLM-000020). Art. 3(3) remains deferred/unminted in batch-1.

3 - Role Model

Controller, processor, joint controller, representative

Definitions and structural gates: controller (EP-CLM-000021), processor (EP-CLM-000022), joint controllers (EP-CLM-000023), Art. 27 representative duty and exceptions (co-render below).

Co-render required: Art. 27(1) representative duty + Art. 27(2) exceptions

EP-CLM-000024 - Where Article 3(2) applies, the controller or the processor shall designate in writing a representative in the Union.

EP-CLM-000025 - The Art. 27(1) designation obligation does not apply to (a) processing that is occasional, does not include, on a large scale, processing of special categories of data referred to in Art. 9(1) or personal data relating to criminal convictions and offences referred to in Art. 10, and is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, taking into account the nature, context, scope and purposes of the processing; or (b) a public authority or body.

Do not cite or render the default without its qualification. See register anchors EP-CLM-000024 and EP-CLM-000025.

4 - Lawfulness + Principles + Accountability

Articles 6 and 5

Lawfulness is a structural gate (EP-CLM-000026) - not a slogan that "consent is required". Principles (EP-CLM-000027) and accountability (EP-CLM-000028) remain distinct claims.

5 - Operational Governance

Articles 24, 25, 28, 30, 32

Controller measures (EP-CLM-000029), design/default (EP-CLM-000030), processor/subprocessor governance (EP-CLM-000031), records default + qualification (co-render), and security (EP-CLM-000034).

Co-render required: Art. 30(1)-(4) records duty + Art. 30(5) <250 qualification (not automatic waiver)

EP-CLM-000032 - Each controller shall maintain a record of processing activities under its responsibility containing Art. 30(1)(a)-(g); each processor shall maintain a record of categories of processing carried out on behalf of a controller containing Art. 30(2)(a)-(d); records shall be in writing including electronic form and made available to the SA on request (Art. 30(3)-(4)).

EP-CLM-000033 - The Art. 30(1)-(2) record obligations do not apply to an enterprise or an organisation employing fewer than 250 persons unless the processing it carries out is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects, the processing is not occasional, or the processing includes special categories of data as referred to in Art. 9(1) or personal data relating to criminal convictions and offences referred to in Art. 10.

Do not cite or render the default without its qualification. See register anchors EP-CLM-000032 and EP-CLM-000033.

6 - Incident Path

Articles 33 and 34

SA notification and data-subject communication are separate defaults, each with mandatory qualification co-render.

Co-render required: Art. 33(1) SA notification + unlikely-risk exception

EP-CLM-000035 - In the case of a personal data breach, the controller shall without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it, notify the personal data breach to the competent supervisory authority under Article 55; if not notified within 72 hours, the notification shall be accompanied by reasons for the delay.

EP-CLM-000036 - Notification to the supervisory authority under Art. 33(1) is not required where the personal data breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

Do not cite or render the default without its qualification. See register anchors EP-CLM-000035 and EP-CLM-000036.

Co-render required: Art. 34(1)-(2) data-subject communication + Art. 34(3) exceptions

EP-CLM-000037 - When a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the controller shall communicate the breach to the data subject without undue delay, in clear and plain language, including at least the information in Art. 33(3)(b)-(d).

EP-CLM-000038 - Communication to the data subject under Art. 34(1) is not required if (a) appropriate technical and organisational protection measures were applied to the affected data (in particular rendering them unintelligible to unauthorised persons, such as encryption); or (b) subsequent measures ensure the high risk is no longer likely to materialise; or (c) it would involve disproportionate effort, in which case there shall instead be a public communication or similar equally effective measure.

Do not cite or render the default without its qualification. See register anchors EP-CLM-000037 and EP-CLM-000038.

7 - DPIA + DPO Triggers

Articles 35 and 37

DPIA high-risk gate and particular cases (EP-CLM-000039). DPO designation is modelled as Art. 37(1)(a)/(b)/(c) triggers (EP-CLM-000040) - not a universal default duty.
