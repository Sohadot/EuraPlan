## 1. What Is Being Acquired

EuraPlan is not a domain name for sale.
EuraPlan is a category position, a governing intelligence architecture, and a proprietary standards layer.

A buyer acquires:

- A premium institutional domain with direct category authority in European regulatory planning
- Named category ownership: **European Regulatory Entry Planning**
- A proprietary entry ontology (EuraPlan Entry Ontology)
- A proprietary readiness standard (European Entry Readiness Standard — EERS)
- A named protocol (EuraPlan Entry Planning Protocol)
- A governed reference architecture (pages, tools, matrices, route registry)
- A high-value, monetisation-ready audience (non-EU tech companies entering Europe)
- A monetisation system built on intelligence outputs, not advertising
- SEO authority in a high-value, structurally low-noise category
- API and standard licensing potential (Phase 4)
- Partnership compatibility with chambers of commerce, trade organisations, law firms, and EU institutions
- Institutional validation pathway potential (Enterprise Europe Network, EIC, DG GROW)

## 2. The Strategic Question

> Can we afford to let EuraPlan remain outside our ownership while it establishes the category language for European regulatory entry planning?

This question applies when a buyer's core business involves:

- Regulatory intelligence or compliance technology
- EU market entry advisory or consulting
- International law practice with EU coverage
- Grant writing, EU funding facilitation, or procurement advisory
- B2B SaaS expansion tooling for international companies
- Chamber of commerce or trade promotion services
- AI governance, risk, or compliance platforms
- European business intelligence or market research

## 4. What Is Not Being Sold

- A website that can be redirected to another purpose without rebuilding the architecture
- A domain squatted on without an intelligence layer
- A template or generic content site

The value is in the governed architecture, the proprietary standards, and the category position — not the domain alone.
