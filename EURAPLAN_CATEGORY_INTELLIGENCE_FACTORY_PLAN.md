## 7. STANDARD

### European Entry Readiness Standard (EERS)

A proprietary EuraPlan standard defining the minimum conditions
for a non-EU company to be considered ready to enter the European market.

**The Eight Readiness Dimensions:**

1. **Regulatory Mapping** — Has the company identified all applicable EU regulations?
2. **Compliance Timeline** — Does the company know its regulatory deadlines?
3. **Country Selection** — Has the target entry country been chosen with evidence?
4. **Entity & Legal Structure** — Is the legal setup for EU operations defined?
5. **Funding Awareness** — Has the company assessed EU funding eligibility?
6. **Product Compliance** — Is the product or service mapped against EU requirements?
7. **Risk Classification** — Are the entry risks identified and categorized?
8. **Execution Plan** — Is there a 30/90/180-day entry plan with concrete steps?

A company scoring below threshold on any dimension
is not entry-ready — regardless of market ambition.

## 8. PROTOCOL

### EuraPlan Entry Planning Protocol

The standard sequence EuraPlan uses to produce entry intelligence:

1. Identify company origin (country / region)
2. Identify entity type (SaaS, AI, IoT, HealthTech, etc.)
3. Identify product or service type
4. Identify regulatory exposure (which EU regulations apply)
5. Identify target EU country or countries
6. Identify sector
7. Identify entry objective
8. Map EU funding and tender relevance
9. Score entry readiness (EERS)
10. Identify planning gaps
11. Produce 30/90/180-day entry plan
12. Map official sources and recommended advisory categories

**This protocol governs all tools, pages, and outputs.**
No intelligence output is valid unless it follows this sequence.
