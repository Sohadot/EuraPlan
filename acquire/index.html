The Strategic Question

Can You Afford to Let This Category Establish Without You?

EuraPlan Buyer Logic — June 2026

This question applies when your organisation's core business intersects with any of the following: regulatory intelligence, EU market entry advisory, international law with EU practice, EU funding facilitation, B2B SaaS expansion tooling, trade promotion, or AI governance and compliance. The category position EuraPlan occupies becomes more expensive to displace with every piece of governed intelligence it publishes and every regulated audience member it serves.

The Asset Architecture

What the Buyer Acquires

  • Category Position Named ownership of the category: European Regulatory Entry Planning. No competitor currently owns the intersection of regulatory intelligence, market entry planning, funding readiness, and non-EU company orientation.
  • Proprietary Standard The European Entry Readiness Standard (EERS) — an eight-dimension proprietary framework defining what it means for a non-EU company to be genuinely entry-ready. Licensable to consulting firms, law firms, and institutional partners.
  • Governing Protocol The EuraPlan Entry Planning Protocol — a twelve-step planning sequence governing intelligence production from company origin to 30/90/180-day entry plan. Deployable as an institutional methodology.
  • Proprietary Ontology The EuraPlan Entry Ontology — a classification system covering entity types, origin regions, regulatory exposures, market targets, entry objectives, and readiness states. The structural backbone of all intelligence production.
  • Governed Reference Architecture A governed set of public pages, routes, and intelligence outputs built on the protocol and ontology. Static-first, SEO-optimised, and structured for Phase 2 interactive tool and Phase 3 diagnostic engine development.
  • High-Value Audience Non-EU AI, SaaS, tech, compliance-sensitive, and growth companies actively planning or considering European market entry — a structurally high-intent, high-value audience for any regulatory intelligence or market entry product.
  • Monetisation Architecture Defined monetisation channels attached to intelligence outputs: paid diagnostic reports, sector entry briefs, funding readiness assessments, expert directory listings, advisory referrals, institutional briefs, API access, and standard licensing. No advertising dependency.
  • Institutional Validation Pathway Structural compatibility with Enterprise Europe Network, European Innovation Council (EIC), DG GROW, and national trade promotion organisations as future institutional partners or validators of the EERS standard.
