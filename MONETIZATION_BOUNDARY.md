## 1. Governing Principle

Every monetization channel must be an extension of the intelligence EuraPlan produces — not a corruption of it.

If a revenue channel requires EuraPlan to produce thinner content, make unsupported claims, feature unvetted companies, or compromise the category claim, it is prohibited.

## 3. Prohibited Channels

| Prohibited | Reason |
|---|---|
| Generic display advertising | Destroys institutional credibility |
| Unvetted affiliate links | Monetization boundary violation |
| Sponsored content without institutional-grade disclosure | Integrity violation |
| Lead selling without full transparency to the user | Trust violation |
| Legal advice framing | Regulatory and reputational risk |
| Unverified company directories | Source policy violation |
| Content farms or SEO spam pages | Category destruction |
| "Partnered with" or "endorsed by" claims without documented agreement | Governance violation |

---

## 4. Advisory Referral Standards

Any advisory referral or expert directory listing requires:

- Named organisation and relevant credentials
- Clear disclosure that EuraPlan receives a referral fee (if applicable)
- No implied endorsement beyond EuraPlan's verification of credentials
- No exclusivity language that creates implied official relationships

---

## 5. Pricing Doctrine

Pricing for intelligence outputs must reflect the value of structured, governed, source-backed planning intelligence — not commodity content pricing.

EuraPlan does not compete on volume or cheapness. It competes on governed intelligence quality.

---

## 6. Channel Activation Requirements

No monetization channel may be activated without:

- A defined intelligence output it is attached to
- Source and claim policy compliance confirmed
- No conflict with the intelligence model or category claim
- Owner approval
