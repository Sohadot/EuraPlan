Asset Identity

What EuraPlan Is

EuraPlan.com is a Category Intelligence Factory. It produces governed, structured, executable planning intelligence for non-EU companies entering the European market. It is not a content website. It is not a consulting firm. It is not a legal advice provider.

EuraPlan Governance Charter — June 2026

EuraPlan produces intelligence outputs governed by a defined protocol, source policy, and claim policy. Its category claim — European Regulatory Entry Planning — is built and defended through a proprietary ontology, a proprietary readiness standard (EERS), a governing protocol, and a governed reference architecture.

Permanent Prohibitions

What EuraPlan Will Not Publish

  • Generic EU content No generic "doing business in Europe" articles. EuraPlan produces planning intelligence for non-EU companies entering the European regulatory system — not orientation content for general audiences.
  • Unverified claims No regulatory deadline, compliance obligation, or funding eligibility claim without a Tier 1 source cited. No statistics without an official source. No claims classified as Pending may be published.
  • Undocumented partnership claims No claim of official partnership with, endorsement by, or approval from any EU institution, national authority, or third-party organisation without a formal, documented agreement.
  • Legal advice framing No language that frames EuraPlan intelligence as legal advice, compliance certification, or a substitute for qualified legal and regulatory counsel. EuraPlan is planning intelligence. Legal counsel is a recommended output of the EuraPlan Entry Planning Protocol.
  • Thin or placeholder pages No page that exists without a defined thesis, target audience, decision problem, and intelligence output. No "coming soon" sections. No content that exists to fill space rather than produce intelligence.
Interface Doctrine

The Interface Must Embody the Asset Thesis

The future EuraPlan interface direction is a European entry control room: regulatory clocks, compliance gates, country matrices, planning pathways, and readiness state layers. This direction is preserved and will be implemented in later phases. The Phase 1 foundation is static-first, accessible, and structurally aligned with the intelligence architecture — not a visual decoration of it.
