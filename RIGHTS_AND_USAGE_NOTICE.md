## 2. Purpose

Prevent mistaken assumptions that public repository visibility, public website access, or due diligence review grants permission to copy, commercialize, republish, or repackage EuraPlan.

---

## 3. Rights posture

EuraPlan.com is a proprietary governed digital asset developed and maintained under **Sohadot** ownership.

Unless a separate written license or transfer agreement is executed by the owner, **all rights in the original EuraPlan system remain reserved**.

---

## 4. No open-source license statement

**No open-source license is granted** by this repository or these governance documents.

Sprint 4G explicitly does **not** add a `LICENSE` file (MIT, Apache, GPL, Creative Commons, or other). No license file should be interpreted as present unless explicitly added by the owner in a future decision recorded in `DECISION_LOG.md`.

---

## 5. Repository visibility note

If the repository is visible to reviewers, contributors, or potential buyers, that visibility is for **governance transparency and due diligence** — not for unrestricted reuse.

Cloning, forking, mirroring, or republishing the corpus for commercial purposes without owner authorization is not permitted by this notice.

---

## 6. Permitted review

The following are permitted for due diligence and acquisition evaluation:

- Reading governance documents and public HTML in the repository
- Verifying git history, decision log, and route registry
- Production header and structured data verification
- Internal buyer team review under confidentiality expectations
- Discussion of strategic acquisition with owner contact (agent@sohadot.com)

---

## 7. Prohibited assumptions

Reviewers must **not** assume:

- Permission to copy or republish the site or governance corpus
- Permission to use the EuraPlan name, brand, or category claim commercially
- That EuraPlan content constitutes legal, tax, compliance, or funding advice
- EU or government endorsement of EuraPlan
- Guaranteed traffic, revenue, ranking, or acquisition outcome
- Automatic transfer of domain, Cloudflare, or registrar accounts through repository access alone

**EuraPlan does not claim ownership** over EU regulations, government publications, or third-party institutional materials.

Reusers of EuraPlan must independently comply with terms governing cited official sources.
