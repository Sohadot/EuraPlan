## 1. Asset Identity

EuraPlan.com is a Category Intelligence Factory.

It produces governed, structured, executable planning intelligence for non-EU companies entering the European market.

It is not a content website. It is not a consulting firm. It is not a legal advice provider.

Every decision — build, content, monetization, partnership — must be evaluated against this identity before execution.

## 2. Governing Sentence

> Europe punishes absence. Europe rewards planning.

## 3. Category Claim

EuraPlan claims ownership of the category: **European Regulatory Entry Planning**.

This category is defined as the structured, intelligence-driven process of mapping, planning, and executing market entry into Europe — accounting for regulatory exposure, country selection, funding readiness, compliance obligations, and national execution differences — before a non-EU company makes its first irreversible move.

This claim is built and defended through:
- The EuraPlan Entry Ontology
- The European Entry Readiness Standard (EERS)
- The EuraPlan Entry Planning Protocol
- The governed reference architecture
- Source-backed intelligence outputs

## 6. Decision Authority

Before any of the following, the governing document must be consulted:

- Publishing a new public page
- Adding a new route
- Making a claim about regulations, funding, country requirements, or timelines
- Entering a partnership or naming an official relationship
- Adding a monetization channel
- Referencing a third party, institution, or authority
- Changing the interface architecture

Any major decision of the kinds listed in DECISION_LOG.md Section 6 must be recorded in DECISION_LOG.md before or during implementation.

**The governing question for every decision:**
> Does this advance the category claim and the intelligence architecture, or does it dilute it?

---

## 7. Prohibited Actions

Permanently prohibited regardless of speed, revenue, or convenience:

- Generic "doing business in Europe" content
- Placeholder or thin pages
- Unverified statistics without official source citation
- Claimed partnerships without formal, documented agreement
- Legal advice framing
- AI chatbot without structured, governed protocol
- Pages without a last-updated date
- Routes that bypass the route governance architecture
- Monetization channels that contradict the intelligence model
